A single posterior cannot cover the full Pareto set when objectives compete. We treat a randomized augmented Chebyshev scalarization as a latent preference, so the posterior becomes a mixture over trade-offs. Elite designs pooled across scalarizations train one diffusion prior on the Pareto-relevant regions. The method avoids acquisition optimization and keeps diversity across trade-offs.
