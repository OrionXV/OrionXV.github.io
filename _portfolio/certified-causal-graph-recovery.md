Identifies the instantaneous, lagged and action-driven causal graph of a linear state-space system from two noisy views of one adaptively controlled trajectory, and abstains when the data cannot tell candidate graphs apart. Recovers the correct graph under heavy sensor noise, where a two-view ICA baseline fails.
