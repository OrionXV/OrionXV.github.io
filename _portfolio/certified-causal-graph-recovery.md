Causal discovery in partially observed dynamical systems must separate structural mechanisms from measurement noise, even when data are collected adaptively. We study bounded linear causal state-space models observed through two noisy views, releasing the second view only after each action so that residuals are free of policy adaptivity. A polynomial cross-view contrast cancels drifting sensor noise, and a denominator-free moment method with instrumental variables yields simultaneous confidence rectangles for the instantaneous, lagged, and action coefficients. Under heavy sensor noise with strongly non-Gaussian sources, the certificate recovers the correct complete graph on all 20 fresh trajectories, while a two-view ICA estimator fails at every budget.
