Molecular campaigns need several strong candidates from a small oracle budget, not just one best molecule. GDA chooses which parent molecules to expand using the mean and spread of their offspring's predicted rewards. Oracle feedback updates the surrogate and the parent pool in a closed loop. On six DOCKSTRING and GEAM tasks, it raises top-1% hit yield by up to 69% over strong baselines.
