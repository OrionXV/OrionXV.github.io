Practical molecular discovery needs several strong candidates within a small oracle budget, not only the single best molecule. Generator Disagreement Acquisition (GDA) chooses which parent molecules to expand using the mean and dispersion of the rewards their offspring are predicted to earn, rather than the parents' own scores. Oracle feedback updates both the surrogate model and the parent pool in a closed loop. Across six DOCKSTRING and GEAM tasks, GDA raises top-1% hit yield by up to 69% over strong baselines.
