About Me

Greetings! I am Arsalaan.

I'm pursuing a Master of Science in Statistics and Data Science at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), Abu Dhabi (Aug 2025 – Present).

Currently, I am working on Bayesian Optimization. My research interests include optimization, generative AI, and reinforcement learning.

Previously, I worked as a Retainer – Petroleum Engineer at ONGC Videsh Ltd. (June 2024 – June 2025) in the Technical Support & Research team, collaborating across functions to optimize operations, automate reporting, and prototype data-driven tools.

I completed my B.Tech in Petroleum Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad (Dec 2020 – May 2024), where I built a strong foundation in machine learning, data science, computer vision, NLP and large language models (LLMs). I contributed to my college's Data Science Club and led Inter-IIT teams as a Machine Learning Engineer.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy web development and creative writing. I previously served as Editor-in-Chief for SPE IIT(ISM) SC and on my school Editorial Board. In my free time, you'll find me reading or tinkering with side projects.
