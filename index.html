About Me

Greetings! I am Arsalaan.

I’m pursuing an MSc in Statistics and Data Science at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), Abu Dhabi (Aug 2025 – Present).

My current research focuses on Bayesian optimization in noisy, sample-limited settings, including Bayesian optimization with generative models and efficient approximations of the Knowledge Gradient acquisition function for high-dimensional problems, under the supervision of Prof. Raúl Astudillo and Prof. Jian Kang. More broadly, I’m interested in probabilistic machine learning, reinforcement learning, LLMs / agentic systems, and time-series modeling for scientific and healthcare applications.

Previously, I worked at ONGC Videsh Ltd. (June 2024 – June 2025) in Technical Support & Research, where I built Python pipelines for large-scale production time-series, designed forecasting and scenario analyses for decision-making, and shipped an internal Django dashboard with ML-based forecasts and anomaly flags.

I completed my B.Tech in Petroleum Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad (Dec 2020 – May 2024), where I developed strong foundations in ML/DL, and worked on projects across recommender systems, multimodal learning, and time-series forecasting (including Kaggle/industry-style settings).

Outside of engineering, I enjoy creative writing. I previously served as Editor-in-Chief for SPE IIT(ISM) SC and on my school Editorial Board. In my free time, you'll find me reading or tinkering with side projects.
