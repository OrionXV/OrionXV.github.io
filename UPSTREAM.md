# Academic Pages

This portfolio uses [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io), based on Minimal Mistakes.

Imported from upstream commit `ea56e24e8aadfdd06f2b42611be9f663290166fa` on 9 September 2026. The original MIT licence is retained in `LICENSE`.

The Jekyll layouts, Sass theme, includes, and supporting assets are from Academic Pages. The sunset design is implemented in `assets/css/sunset.css`, with customized navigation, profile, banner, footer, and project templates. Demo biographies, publications, and other sample content were not imported.

The previous website is preserved under `legacy/` from portfolio commit `3f982c18fb6d11ba67fb9021ca7a9e7c98b8e052`.
